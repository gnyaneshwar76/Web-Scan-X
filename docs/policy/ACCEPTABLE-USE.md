# WebScanX Acceptable Use Policy

Operator: gnyaneshwar76 (the WebScanX project). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL - owner to fill]

## The rule
Scan only what you own, or what you have written permission to test. Stay inside that permission.

## Allowed
- Your own websites and your own source code.
- Systems where the owner gave you written permission, for the scope and dates they gave.

- App files (Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac) that you own, or that you have the right to analyze.

## Forbidden
- Scanning any system you do not own and have no written permission to test.
- Going outside your permitted scope (other hosts, paths, times).
- Denial of service: using the tool to overload or crash a target.
- Evading or removing the authorization gate, or ticking it when it is not true.
- Attacking, harassing or harming third parties, or using findings to do so.
- Using findings to steal, change or destroy data, or to extort anyone.
- Breaking the law where you or the target are located.

## App files
Analyze only app files you own or have the right to analyze. The licence or terms of a third-party app may forbid reverse engineering or analysis, even though WebScanX only reads the file and does not run it. You are responsible for checking those terms. ## Aggressive mode (`--aggressive`)
Off by default. When on, it can send requests that change or delete data, slow the target, or trigger alarms and blocking. Use it only on systems you own or where your written permission clearly allows it, and ideally on a test copy with backups. You carry all risk.

## Consequences
If you break this policy, we may end your permission to use WebScanX under the Terms. Unauthorized scanning can also be a crime and can lead to civil claims against you. We may cooperate with lawful requests from authorities. The authors are not responsible for your use.

## Questions
Not sure if you are allowed to scan something? Do not scan it. Ask the owner first.
